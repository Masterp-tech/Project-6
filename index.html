Limitations of BMI

Although BMI is often a practical indicator of weight in relation to height, it is not suited for every person. Some groups should carefully consider their BMI results, and in certain cases the measurement may not be useful on its own.

♀

Gender

Body composition can vary with age and development. BMI should be interpreted in context rather than used as a complete picture of health.

♙

Age

Body composition changes as people age, so BMI may not fully reflect body composition in older adults.

♧

Muscle

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, so it may not reflect the health of people with higher muscle mass.

◉

Pregnancy

Pregnancy changes weight and body composition. BMI should be discussed with a healthcare professional during pregnancy.

♙

Race and ethnicity

Health risks can vary across populations, and the same BMI may not mean the same thing for everyone.
